About This lab

This lab follows the principles of Miyamoto Musashi — ken-do (the way of the sword) applied to learning. No fluff, no distraction. Only direct mastery.

The Dokkodo (Way of Walking Alone)

  • Accept everything just as it is.
  • Do not seek pleasure for its own sake.
  • Do not, under any circumstances, depend on a partial feeling.
  • Think lightly of yourself and deeply of the world.
